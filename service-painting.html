Good paint work is mostly good prep. I take the time to patch, sand, caulk, and prime so the finish looks clean and holds up, not just until the paint dries.

Inside, that means walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinets. Outside, it's siding, trim, porches, and doors, using products made to stand up to Indiana weather. Whether it's a single room or the whole house, I treat it like it's my own.

What I handle

  • Ceilings, trim, doors & cabinet repainting
  • Exterior siding, trim, porches & doors
  • Accent walls & color changes
  • Careful prep, priming & daily cleanup
