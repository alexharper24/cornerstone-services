Meet the Owner

Hi, I'm Stephen Williams.

Husband, dad, and your Warsaw neighbor.

I grew up one of ten kids out in the country, the kind of upbringing where you learn early to work hard, do a job right, and treat people the way you'd want to be treated. When I'm not working, you'll usually find me with my family or out in the woods.

Most of what I do is painting, inside and out, along with drywall repairs and the small remodels and fixes that tend to sit on a to-do list for months. No job is too small, and I'll always give you my honest take on what's worth doing.

I like to do things decently and in order: careful prep, clean lines, fair prices, and a job site left tidy at the end of the day. When you hire Cornerstone, you're not getting a big crew that rushes in and out. You're getting a neighbor who cares how it turns out and stands behind his work.
