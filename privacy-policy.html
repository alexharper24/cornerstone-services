Privacy

Privacy Policy

What happens to the information you send through this site. It is short because the site does very little.

Last updated 5 September 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into the estimate form: your name, your email address, your phone number if you give one, and whatever you tell us about the project. Nothing else.

What we do with it

It gets emailed to Stephen so he can reply to you and give you an estimate. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone else, and we do not send marketing emails.

How the form works

The form is handled by Formspree, a service that takes the submission and forwards it as an email. Your message passes through their systems on the way to Stephen's inbox, and it is kept in his email after that.

Tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no third-party scripts. It sets no cookies. Nothing here follows you to other websites. The fonts and images are served from this site rather than from anyone else's.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, their servers keep standard request logs, which can include your IP address. That is GitHub's system rather than ours and we do not have access to it.

Photos

The project photos on this site are of work Cornerstone has done. If your home appears in one and you would rather it did not, say so and it comes down.

Asking us about your information

If you want to know what we have, or want it deleted, email cornerstone22022@gmail.com or call (574) 377-0573 and it will be handled.
