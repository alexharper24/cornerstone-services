Most repairs are really about matching what's already there, the texture, the level, and the finish, so the fix blends in instead of standing out.

I handle the small stuff that isn't worth a big contractor's time and trouble: a doorknob hole, settling cracks, nail pops, or a patch after a leak. I finish and sand it smooth so it's ready for paint and you'd never know it was there.

What I handle

  • Water-damage patching
  • Texture matching
  • Finishing & sanding, ready to paint
